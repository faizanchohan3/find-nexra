Questions

Does it send emails to suppliers for me?

No. It writes the RFQ and negotiation emails; you copy them and send from your own inbox, so suppliers always deal with you directly.

Can it change my Amazon listings?

No. The Amazon connection on the Store Manager plan is read-only — it audits listings and diagnoses sales. You make any changes in Seller Central.

Will it tell me if a product is profitable?

Research gives you profit and ROI estimates, and the PPC plan works out your break-even ACoS. These are estimates — confirm fees in Amazon's revenue calculator before you order stock.

Are the suppliers it suggests verified?

No. It points you to where to search and gives you a checklist to verify each supplier yourself — samples, documents, references and payment terms.

The compliance check is free for everyone, plus 5 product searches a day on the Free plan. AI Store Manager runs start with Elite.

Questions

Do I need an Amazon seller account to use findNexra?

No. Research and the AI Store Manager work without one. You only need Seller Central to connect your store on the Store Manager plan.

Where does the product data come from?

Live Amazon data through Keepa. Monthly sales use Amazon's “bought in past month” figure when available; otherwise they're estimated from sales rank and marked with *.

Is the profit figure exact?

No — it's an estimate. FBA fees come from Keepa when available, otherwise they're estimated. Confirm with Amazon's revenue calculator before you order stock.

Does it send emails or change my listings?

No. Supplier emails are drafts you send yourself, and the Amazon connection is read-only.

Can I search in Roman Urdu?

Yes. Type your request in English or Roman Urdu and the AI turns it into filters.

How do I upgrade or cancel?

Buy a plan on nexrallc.com using the same email as your findNexra account — it switches on automatically. Plans renew monthly; cancel anytime from your Nexra account.

Is findNexra part of Amazon?

No. findNexra is made by Nexra LLC and isn't affiliated with Amazon. Figures are estimates for research only.

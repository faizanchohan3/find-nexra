Questions, answered

Can I start for free?

Yes. Create an account with your email — no card needed. The Free plan gives you 5 searches a day and the compliance check.

How do I upgrade?

Choose a plan to buy it on nexrallc.com. Check out with the same email as your findNexra account and the plan switches on automatically. If you buy first, it's applied when you sign up.

Do plans renew automatically?

Yes. Paid plans renew every month until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your Nexra account on nexrallc.com.

What's the difference between Pro and Store Manager?

Pro includes 5 AI Store Manager runs a day. Store Manager raises that to 30 a day and adds the Amazon store connection, listing audits and sales diagnosis.

Are the sales and profit numbers exact?

No — they're estimates for research. Confirm Amazon fees in Amazon's revenue calculator before you order stock.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Buy the plan you want on nexrallc.com with the same email and your account moves to it.
